## 1. Purpose

This Security Awareness Training Tracker provides a template for monitoring and documenting security training completion across the organization. Effective security training is critical for reducing human-factor risks and ensuring policy compliance.

## 3. Training Delivery Methods

| Method | Use Cases | Advantages | Disadvantages |
|--------|-----------|------------|---------------|
| **Online LMS (Learning Management System)** | Annual required training, self-paced modules | Scalable, trackable, completion reporting | Less engaging, passive learning |
| **Instructor-Led Virtual Training** | Role-based training, technical deep-dives | Interactive, Q&A, hands-on exercises | Scheduling challenges, time-intensive |
| **Phishing Simulations** | Quarterly phishing tests | Realistic, measures behavior change | Can cause anxiety, requires careful messaging |
| **Lunch & Learn Sessions** | Optional awareness topics, emerging threats | Informal, discussion-based, team building | Optional attendance, not trackable |
| **Micro-Training (Videos/Infographics)** | Just-in-time learning, policy updates | Quick, visual, mobile-friendly | Limited depth, requires reinforcement |
| **On-the-Job Training** | New hire onboarding, tool-specific training | Practical, immediate application | Inconsistent, not standardized |

**Recommended LMS Platforms:**
- Microsoft Learn for Azure/M365 certifications
- KnowBe4 for security awareness and phishing simulations
- SANS Security Awareness for comprehensive training library
- Moodle (open-source) for budget-conscious organizations

## 5. Training Content Library

### 5.1 Core Training Modules (Recommended Curriculum)

**Module 1: Security Awareness Fundamentals (30 minutes)**
- Why security matters (business impact of breaches)
- Common cyber threats (phishing, ransomware, malware)
- Your role in security (human firewall concept)
- Company security policies overview
- Reporting incidents and getting help
- Quiz: 10 questions (80% passing score)

**Module 2: Phishing & Social Engineering (20 minutes)**
- What is phishing? (email, SMS, phone calls)
- Identifying phishing emails (red flags, examples)
- What to do if you receive phishing email (report, don't click)
- Social engineering tactics (pretexting, urgency, authority)
- Real-world case studies (BEC, CEO fraud)
- Interactive simulation examples
- Quiz: 10 questions (80% passing score)

**Module 3: Data Classification & Handling (20 minutes)**
- Data classification levels (Public, Internal, Confidential, Highly Confidential, Restricted)
- How to apply sensitivity labels in Microsoft 365
- Sharing data securely (OneDrive links, not email attachments)
- GDPR personal data basics
- Data retention and disposal
- Examples by department
- Quiz: 10 questions (80% passing score)

**Module 4: Password & MFA Best Practices (15 minutes)**
- Creating strong passwords (passphrases, password manager)
- Never share passwords (even with IT)
- How MFA works and why it's required
- Enrolling in Microsoft Authenticator
- Recognizing MFA fatigue attacks
- Protecting credentials
- Quiz: 10 questions (80% passing score)

**Module 5: Remote Work Security (20 minutes)**
- Securing home Wi-Fi (WPA2/WPA3, strong password)
- Physical security (lock screen, privacy, shred documents)
- Public Wi-Fi risks and VPN usage
- Device security (encryption, updates, antivirus)
- Separating work and personal use
- Video conferencing security
- Quiz: 10 questions (80% passing score)

**Report Contents:**
- Executive summary (1 paragraph, overall posture)

- Budget and resource needs for upcoming quarter
- Recommendations for policy or process changes

## 7. Enforcement and Remediation

### 7.1 Compliance Enforcement

**Non-Compliance Actions:**

| Scenario | Action | Timeline |
|----------|--------|----------|
| **New hire overdue (>14 days)** | Email reminder to employee + manager | Day 15 |
| **New hire overdue (>21 days)** | Manager escalation, training required before system access | Day 22 |
| **Annual refresher overdue (>30 days)** | Email reminder to employee + manager | 30 days past due |
| **Annual refresher overdue (>60 days)** | System access restricted until training complete | 60 days past due |
| **Failed phishing simulation (1st time)** | Micro-training sent automatically | Immediately |
| **Failed phishing simulation (2nd time)** | Manager notification + remedial training required | Within 24 hours |
| **Failed phishing simulation (3+ times)** | Formal written warning + in-person training | Within 48 hours |
| **Entered credentials in phishing simulation** | Immediate password reset + manager notification + remedial training | Immediately |

### 7.2 Remedial Training

**For repeat phishing failures or policy violations:**
- **Content:** 30-minute in-person or virtual training session
- **Delivery:** IT Security Team or HR (one-on-one or small group)
- **Topics:** Review of mistake, why it matters, how to avoid future failures
- **Documentation:** Training completion logged, manager notified
- **Follow-Up:** Additional phishing simulation within 30 days to verify improvement

### 7.3 Positive Reinforcement

**Recognition for Security Champions:**
- **Monthly Award:** "Security Awareness Champion" (employee who reported most phishing emails)
- **Quarterly Award:** Department with highest compliance rate (certificate, small prize)
- **Annual Award:** "Cybersecurity Employee of the Year" (recognized at company meeting)
- **Incentives:** Gift cards, extra PTO day, public recognition

## 8. Training Maintenance & Improvement

### 8.1 Annual Training Review

**Evaluate and update training content:**
- [ ] Review industry threat landscape (new attack types, emerging threats)
- [ ] Analyze incident data (which incidents were training-related?)
- [ ] Gather user feedback (surveys, post-training evaluations)
- [ ] Update examples and screenshots (current Office UI, company branding)
- [ ] Refresh videos and interactive content (avoid staleness)
- [ ] Benchmark against industry best practices (KnowBe4, SANS)
- [ ] Update compliance requirements (new regulations, policy changes)

### 8.2 Continuous Improvement

**Metrics-Driven Enhancements:**
- If phishing click rate >15%: Increase simulation frequency, improve training content
- If training completion <90%: Simplify enrollment, reduce module length, improve reminders
- If role-based training low: Make training more relevant, tie to job performance reviews
- If repeat offenders >10: Implement mandatory in-person training, manager accountability

**User Feedback Integration:**
- Post-training surveys (1-minute, 3 questions: helpful?, engaging?, suggestions?)
- Focus groups (quarterly, 5-8 participants from different departments)
- Manager feedback (Are employees changing behaviors?)

## 9. Integration with Performance Reviews

**Annual Performance Review Inclusion:**
- Security training completion (met/not met expectations)
- Phishing simulation performance (number of clicks, reports)
- Policy compliance (acknowledging updates, following procedures)
- Security incident involvement (reported promptly? caused by negligence?)

**Manager Guidance:**
- Positive Example: "John completed all security training on time and consistently reports phishing emails, demonstrating strong security awareness."
- Needs Improvement: "Jane failed 3 phishing simulations this year. Remedial training required. Expect improvement in next review period."

## Notes for Implementation

1. **Start Simple:** If this is your first training tracker, start with basic spreadsheet tracking before investing in expensive LMS platforms

2. **Automate Where Possible:** Use Power Automate or similar tools to reduce manual tracking burden

3. **Enforce Consistently:** Apply compliance enforcement equally across all levels (including executives)

4. **Celebrate Successes:** Recognize employees who demonstrate strong security awareness (positive reinforcement)

5. **Iterate Quarterly:** Review training effectiveness every quarter and adjust content, frequency, or delivery methods

6. **Measure Behavior Change:** Training completion is not the goal—behavior change is. Track security incidents and phishing click rates as true measures of success.
