## Completed Presentations

### 01: Executive Briefing - Security Investment ✓

**Purpose:** Secure executive sponsorship and budget approval for SMB Security Framework

**Slides:** 23 + 6 appendix slides

**Key Content:**
- SMB threat landscape (43% of attacks target SMBs, 60% don't survive breach)
- Current security posture assessment (baseline Secure Score, gaps)
- Proposed 4-phase framework (timeline, effort, automation)
- Budget breakdown (investment vs. breach cost comparison)
- ROI analysis (55% time savings, breach prevention value)
- Risk of inaction (regulatory, competitive, financial)
- Success metrics (Secure Score, MFA adoption, phishing rates)
- Call to action (approve budget, endorse framework)

**Deliverables:**
- PowerPoint deck (customizable template)
- Executive summary handout (1-page)
- Detailed proposal document (supporting material)

**Presenter Notes:** Included for each slide with talking points, analogies, and response to anticipated questions

**When to Use:**
- Board meeting (quarterly or special session)
- Executive team strategy session
- Budget approval meetings
- Annual security planning

## Presentation Templates (To Be Developed)

### 02: Department Head Security Responsibilities

**Purpose:** Train managers on their security roles and how to support the framework

**Target Audience:**
- Department heads (Sales, Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations)
- Team leads and supervisors
- Project managers

**Duration:** 60 minutes (45 min presentation + 15 min Q&A)

**Key Topics:**
1. Why Managers Are Critical to Security Success (10 min)
   - Setting tone from the top
   - Enforcing policies within team
   - Balancing security and productivity

2. Manager Responsibilities (20 min)
   - Approve user access requests
   - Quarterly access reviews
   - Ensure team completes training
   - Model good security behavior
   - Support incident response

3. Common Scenarios & How to Handle (15 min)
   - Employee asks to use unapproved cloud service
   - Team member clicks phishing simulation
   - Customer requests data sharing
   - Employee resignation (access revocation)

4. Tools & Resources (10 min)
   - RACI matrix (who does what)
   - Policies and quick references
   - Escalation procedures
   - IT support contacts

5. Q&A and Discussion (15 min)

**Format:** Interactive with case studies, role-playing scenarios

### 03: All-Hands Cybersecurity Awareness

**Purpose:** Launch security framework to entire organization, build awareness and buy-in

**Target Audience:** All employees (company-wide meeting)

**Duration:** 30 minutes (25 min presentation + 5 min Q&A)

**Key Topics:**
1. Why This Matters (5 min)
   - Real incidents from similar SMBs
   - Business impact (we all lose jobs if breach causes closure)
   - Our role as human firewall

2. What's Changing (10 min)
   - MFA required (within 7 days—show enrollment process)
   - Security training (annual requirement)
   - Sensitivity labels (demonstrate in Outlook)
   - Phishing simulations (quarterly practice)

3. What We're Doing to Support You (5 min)
   - Training videos and quick reference guides
   - IT Help Desk support (phone/email)
   - FAQs on intranet
   - In-person assistance available

4. Your Action Items (3 min)
   - Enroll in MFA this week
   - Complete training modules (2 hours total)
   - Apply sensitivity labels starting now
   - Report suspicious emails (use button)

5. Q&A (5 min)
   - "What if I lose my phone?"
   - "Will this slow me down?"
   - "What happens if I fail phishing test?"

**Tone:** Positive, empowering (not fear-based), practical

**Visuals:** Real examples, employee testimonials, demo videos

## Production Guidelines

### PowerPoint/Keynote Specifications
- **Slide Size:** 16:9 widescreen (1920x1080)
- **Template:** Company-branded template with logo
- **Fonts:** Company standard fonts (fallback: Arial, Calibri)
- **Colors:** Brand color palette
- **File Format:** .pptx (PowerPoint), .key (Keynote), .pdf (handouts)

### Accessibility
- High contrast slides (dark text on light background)
- Alt text for all images and charts
- Minimum font size: 24pt body, 32pt+ headings
- Avoid red/green only distinctions (colorblind-friendly)
- Provide transcript/notes for hearing impaired

### Presenter Resources
- **Speaker Notes:** Detailed talking points for each slide
- **Timing Guide:** Recommended minutes per slide
- **Handouts:** Executive summary, key takeaways (1-2 pages)
- **Demo Scripts:** Step-by-step for live demos (MFA, labeling)
- **Q&A Prep:** Anticipated questions with suggested answers

## Delivery Best Practices

### Before Presentation
- **Audience Analysis:** Tailor content to audience technical level
- **Dry Run:** Rehearse 2-3 times, check timing
- **Tech Check:** Test projector, audio, demo accounts
- **Backup Plan:** PDF on USB drive (if tech fails)
- **Print Handouts:** One per attendee

### During Presentation
- **Start Strong:** Grab attention with compelling stat or story
- **Eye Contact:** Avoid reading slides
- **Pace:** Slow down, allow pauses for absorption
- **Engagement:** Ask questions, poll audience, use real examples
- **Time Management:** Stay on schedule, allow time for Q&A

### After Presentation
- **Distribute Materials:** Email deck + handouts within 24 hours
- **Follow-Up:** Capture action items, assign owners
- **Feedback:** Survey attendees (what worked, what to improve)
- **Record:** Video record for employees who couldn't attend

## Budget Considerations

**DIY Approach:**
- Use company-branded PowerPoint template (free)
- Internal presenters (IT Director, CISO) (no cost)
- Print handouts in-house (minimal cost)
**Total Cost:** <£100

**Professional Approach:**
- Hire presentation designer for custom graphics (£500-£1,000)
- Professional speaker/trainer (£1,000-£3,000 per day)
- Video recording and editing (£500-£1,500)
**Total Cost:** £2,000-£5,500

**Recommended for SMBs:** DIY for Presentations 02-03, consider professional support for Presentation 01 (high-stakes executive audience)
