## Completed Scripts

### Video 01: Why Cybersecurity Matters for SMBs ✓
**Learning Objectives:**
- Understand SMB threat landscape (60% fail after breach, 43% of attacks target SMBs)
- Recognize business impact (financial, operational, reputational)
- Learn common threats (phishing, ransomware, BEC, insider threats)
- Accept personal responsibility as "human firewall"

### Video 02: Setting Up Multi-Factor Authentication ✓
**Learning Objectives:**
- Enroll in Microsoft Authenticator app (step-by-step)
- Understand MFA methods (push notification, FIDO2, TOTP)
- Test MFA setup and daily usage
- Troubleshoot common issues

### Video 04: Recognizing Phishing Emails ✓
**Learning Objectives:**
- Identify 10 phishing red flags
- Recognize phishing types (credential, BEC, spear phishing, vishing, smishing)
- Report phishing using Outlook button
- Respond appropriately if compromised

## Video Script Templates (To Be Developed)

### Video 03: Creating Strong Passwords
**Key Topics:**
- Passphrase method ("Coffee-Morning-Sunshine-2025")
- Password manager usage (company-approved tool)
- Avoiding common mistakes (personal info, reuse, dictionary words)
- Password policy compliance (12+ characters, no expiration)
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes

### Video 05: Classifying and Labeling Documents
**Key Topics:**
- 5-tier classification (Public, Internal, Confidential, Highly Confidential, Restricted)
- Applying sensitivity labels in Word, Excel, Outlook
- Auto-labeling features
- Sharing confidential data securely (OneDrive links, not email)
**Duration:** 5-6 minutes

### Video 06: Secure Remote Work Practices
**Key Topics:**
- Home Wi-Fi security (WPA2/WPA3, strong password)
- Physical security (lock screen, privacy screens, shredding)
- Public Wi-Fi risks and VPN usage
- Video conferencing security (virtual backgrounds, screen sharing)
**Duration:** 6-7 minutes

### Video 07: Mobile Device Security
**Key Topics:**
- Device passcode/biometric lock
- Company-owned vs. BYOD (MAM vs. MDM)
- Approved apps from Company Portal
- Lost/stolen device reporting
**Duration:** 5-6 minutes

### Video 08: Reporting Security Incidents
**Key Topics:**
- What qualifies as incident (malware, phishing, lost device, data spill)
- How to report (phone, email, report button)
- Whom to contact (IT Help Desk, Security Team, after-hours)
- What happens after you report
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes

### Video 09: Safe Cloud Service Usage
**Key Topics:**
- Approved vs. unapproved services (shadow IT risks)
- How to request new cloud service
- Securing OneDrive/SharePoint (external sharing, links)
- Personal cloud storage prohibition (Dropbox, Google Drive for work files)
**Duration:** 5-6 minutes

### Video 10: Data Backup and Recovery
**Key Topics:**
- What's backed up (OneDrive, SharePoint, email)
- What's NOT backed up (local hard drive, unapproved cloud)
- How to restore deleted files (recycle bin, version history)
- Importance of saving to OneDrive/SharePoint
**Duration:** 4-5 minutes

## Production Guidelines

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p HD)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps
- **Bitrate:** 5-10 Mbps
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9
- **Closed Captions:** SRT file format, burned-in or selectable

### Branding
- Company logo in corner throughout video
- Consistent color scheme matching company branding
- Professional title cards with company fonts
- Contact information slide at end of each video

### Accessibility
- Closed captions/subtitles (required)
- Transcript downloadable as PDF
- Audio description for visually impaired (if required by policy)
- Minimum font size: 24pt for on-screen text
- High contrast (avoid low-contrast color combinations)

### Quality Standards
- Professional voiceover (clear, conversational tone)
- Pacing: ~150 words per minute (allows time to absorb)
- Background music: Subtle, non-distracting (or none)
- Screen recordings: Smooth, no cursor jumps
- Animations: Simple, purposeful (not flashy)

## Delivery & Distribution

### Learning Management System (LMS)
- Upload to company LMS with metadata (title, description, tags)
- Assign to user groups (All Employees, Remote Workers, Mobile Users)
- Track completion and quiz results
- Automated reminders for incomplete training

### On-Demand Access
- Company intranet video library
- Searchable by topic
- Related materials linked (policies, quick references)

### Mandatory vs. Optional
**Mandatory (Annual, all employees):**
- Video 01: Why Cybersecurity Matters
- Video 02: Setting Up MFA
- Video 03: Creating Strong Passwords
- Video 04: Recognizing Phishing
- Video 08: Reporting Incidents

**Conditional (Role-based):**
- Video 05: Data Classification (All employees handling Confidential+ data)
- Video 06: Remote Work (Remote workers)
- Video 07: Mobile Security (Mobile device users)
- Video 09: Cloud Services (All employees, optional)
- Video 10: Backup & Recovery (Optional, good practice)

## Post-Video Quizzes

Each video includes 5-question quiz:
- Multiple choice format
- 80% passing score (4/5 correct)
- Unlimited attempts
- Immediate feedback with explanations
- Completion tracked in LMS

## Follow-Up Micro-Learning

**Monthly Security Tips (2-minute videos):**
- Phishing examples from real attempts blocked by IT
- New threat alerts (seasonal scams, trending attacks)
- Policy updates
- Success stories (employees who caught phishing)
- Cybersecurity news relevant to SMBs

## Metrics & Effectiveness

**Track:**
- Completion rates by department
- Average quiz scores
- Time to complete (identify too-long videos)
- Phishing simulation click rates (before/after training)
- Security incidents attributed to lack of training

**Target Goals:**
- 95% completion rate within 30 days of hire
- Average quiz score >85%
- Phishing click rate <10% (down from typical 25-30%)
- Zero incidents due to training gap

## Budget Considerations

**DIY Production (Low Budget):**
- Screen recording: OBS Studio (free) or Camtasia ($300)
- Video editing: DaVinci Resolve (free) or Adobe Premiere
- Voiceover: Internal employee or freelance (Fiverr, Upwork)
- Stock footage/music: Envato Elements ($16/month)
**Estimated Cost:** $500-$1,500 for all 10 videos

**Professional Production (Higher Quality):**
- Hire video production company
- Professional scriptwriting, voiceover, animation
- Custom graphics and branding
**Estimated Cost:** $500-$2,000 per video ($5,000-$20,000 total)

**Recommended Approach for SMBs:**
- DIY Videos 01-04 (critical, deploy quickly)
- Professional production for Videos 05-10 (if budget allows)
- Refresh annually or after major policy changes

## Continuous Improvement

**Annual Review:**
- Update statistics and examples
- Refresh screenshots (UI changes in Microsoft 365)
- Incorporate real incidents from past year
- Add new threats (AI-generated phishing, deepfakes)
- Gather user feedback (post-video surveys)

**Feedback Collection:**
- Post-video survey (3 questions):
  1. Was this training helpful? (1-5 scale)
  2. Was the pacing appropriate? (Too fast / Just right / Too slow)
  3. Suggestions for improvement? (Free text)
