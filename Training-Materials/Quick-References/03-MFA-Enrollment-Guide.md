# MFA Enrollment Guide

**Set Up Multi-Factor Authentication in 5 Minutes | Step-by-Step with Screenshots**

---

## ⏱️ Quick Start (3 Steps)

1. **Download** Microsoft Authenticator app (iPhone or Android)
2. **Enroll** at https://aka.ms/mfasetup
3. **Test** by signing out and back in

**Deadline:** Within 7 days of account creation

---

## 📱 Step 1: Download Microsoft Authenticator

### iPhone (iOS):
1. Open **App Store**
2. Search: **"Microsoft Authenticator"**
3. Tap **"Get"** then **"Install"**
4. Open app (blue shield icon)

### Android:
1. Open **Google Play Store**
2. Search: **"Microsoft Authenticator"**
3. Tap **"Install"**
4. Open app (blue shield icon)

**Cost:** Free (no in-app purchases)

---

## 🔐 Step 2: Enroll Your Account

### On Your Computer:

**A. Navigate to MFA Setup Page**
1. Open browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)
2. Go to: **https://aka.ms/mfasetup**
3. Sign in with:
   - Email: your.name@company.com
   - Password: [your current password]
4. Click **"Next"** when prompted

**B. Add Account to Authenticator App**
1. On computer: Click **"Next"** (QR code appears)
2. On phone: Open Microsoft Authenticator app
3. Tap **"+"** (top right)
4. Tap **"Work or school account"**
5. Tap **"Scan QR code"**
6. Point camera at QR code on computer screen
7. Account automatically added ✓

**C. Complete Registration**
1. On phone: Notification appears "Approve sign-in?"
2. **Verify number matches** on computer and phone
3. Tap **"Approve"**
4. On computer: "You're all set!" message appears

**D. Add Backup Method (Important!)**
1. On computer: Click **"I want to set up a different method"**
2. Select **"Phone"**
3. Enter mobile number: +44 [your number]
4. Click **"Next"**
5. Receive SMS code
6. Enter 6-digit code
7. Click **"Next"**
8. Backup method confirmed ✓

---

## ✅ Step 3: Test Your Setup

1. **Sign out** of Microsoft 365
2. **Sign back in:**
   - Email: your.name@company.com
   - Password: [your password]
3. **Approve MFA prompt on phone:**
   - Notification appears on phone
   - Verify number matches
   - Tap "Approve"
4. **Success!** You're signed in

---

## 💡 Daily Usage

### Normal Sign-In Process:
1. Enter email + password on computer
2. Notification on phone: "Approve sign-in?"
3. **Check number matches** (e.g., "75")
4. Tap "Approve"
5. Signed in! (takes ~5 seconds)

### Trusted Devices:
- On your company laptop: Check "Don't ask again for 30 days"
- MFA still required every 30 days (security refresh)
- Always required on new/unknown devices

## ❌ Common Mistakes to Avoid

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Approve MFA prompts you didn't initiate
- ❌ Share MFA codes with anyone (including IT support)
- ❌ Use SMS as primary method (less secure, use as backup only)
- ❌ Delete app without unenrolling first
- ❌ Ignore number matching (always verify)

**DO:**
- ✓ Keep phone unlocked with biometric (Face ID, fingerprint)
- ✓ Register 2+ MFA methods (Authenticator + SMS backup)
- ✓ Report lost phone within 1 hour
- ✓ Deny unexpected MFA prompts and report

## 🔢 Alternative: Backup Codes (Manual Entry)

**If push notifications don't work:**

1. Open Microsoft Authenticator app
2. Tap account name
3. 6-digit code appears (changes every 30 seconds)
4. On computer: Click "I can't use my Microsoft Authenticator app right now"
5. Select "Use a verification code from my mobile app"
6. Enter 6-digit code from app
7. Click "Verify"

**This is your backup method if phone has no internet connection**

## 🎯 Why MFA Matters

**MFA blocks 99.9% of automated account takeover attempts**

Without MFA:
- Attacker steals your password (phishing, breach database)
- Attacker signs in as you
- Full access to email, files, company systems

With MFA:
- Attacker steals your password
- Attacker tries to sign in
- MFA prompt sent to YOUR phone
- You deny it and report
- Attack blocked ✓

**5 minutes to enroll = 99.9% protection**
