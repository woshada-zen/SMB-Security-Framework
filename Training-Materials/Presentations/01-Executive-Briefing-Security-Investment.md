## Slide 4: Why SMBs Are Targeted

**Three Key Reasons:**

1. **Easier Targets**
   - Often lack dedicated security teams
   - Limited investment in security tools
   - Automated attacks scan for vulnerabilities indiscriminately

2. **Valuable Data**
   - Customer information
   - Financial data
   - Intellectual property
   - Access to larger partners (supply chain attacks)

3. **Less Prepared**
   - No incident response plans
   - Infrequent backups (or untested backups)
   - Limited security awareness training

**Phase 0: Baseline Assessment (Weeks 1-2)**
- Document current Secure Score
- Complete security questionnaire
- Secure executive sponsorship ✓ (this meeting)
- Effort: 4-6 hours

**Phase 1: Identity Foundation (Weeks 3-6)**
- MFA for all users (99.9% attack prevention)
- Conditional Access policies
- Password protection & SSPR
- Effort: 8-12 hours | Time Savings: 5-7 hours

**Phase 2: Endpoint Protection (Weeks 7-12)**
- Defender for Endpoint rollout
- Device compliance policies
- Attack Surface Reduction (ASR) rules
- Effort: 6-10 hours | Time Savings: 6-9 hours

**Phase 3: Data Governance (Weeks 13-20)**
- Sensitivity labels (5-tier taxonomy)
- DLP policies (GDPR, financial, IP protection)
- Retention & compliance
- Effort: 10-15 hours | Time Savings: 7-9 hours

**Phase 4: Security Monitoring (Weeks 21-26)**
- Azure Sentinel SIEM
- 20 analytics rules for threat detection
- Automated incident response
- Effort: 8-12 hours | Time Savings: 6-8 hours

**Total Effort:** 36-55 hours (vs. 67-96 hours manual = 55% time savings)

## Presentation Delivery Tips

**Preparation:**
- Rehearse 2-3 times (timing, flow, transitions)
- Anticipate tough questions (budget, ROI, timeline)
- Have detailed backup slides ready
- Print handouts (executive summary + budget)

**Delivery:**
- Start strong (threat landscape stats grab attention)
- Focus on business impact, not technical details
- Use analogies (e.g., "MFA is like two-factor for your bank account")
- Make it personal ("How would you feel if we lost customer data?")
- End with clear call to action (approve budget today)

**Follow-Up:**
- Send presentation deck + detailed proposal within 24 hours
- Schedule follow-up meeting if decision deferred
- Begin Phase 0 immediately upon approval
